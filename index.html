Multi-Fidelity GP Surrogate for 2D Heat Conduction

A 2D transient finite-difference heat solver in pure PyTorch (Dirichlet boundaries) at two resolutions, coarse (16×16) and fine (64×64). A GPyTorch Exact GP couples a Matérn-5/2 kernel over six heater parameters with an IndexKernel over fidelity levels. A sequential uncertainty-per-cost active-learning loop chooses which fidelity to query, reaching 0.0076°C absolute error on a held-out setup.

Acoustic Vector Identification

Undergraduate research supervised by Dr. J. A. Bala exploring acoustic identification of disease vectors from wingbeat frequency spectra. Formulated a 48-dimensional feature-extraction pipeline in Python (MFCCs, spectral centroid, spectral rolloff, zero-crossing rate) across 2,400 balanced Wingbeats recordings, and trained a lightweight PyTorch baseline classifier (VectorNet, 15.3k parameters, ~68 KB checkpoint) achieving 73.89% accuracy on a stratified test split.
