BuildHub — Bridging Africa's Hardware Engineering Gap

I founded BuildHub at FUTMinna because I saw too many talented engineering students stuck in pure textbook theory. We run hands-on cohorts where students wire physical circuits, program microcontrollers, and learn circuit simulation.

To scale our impact beyond our campus, we record open video tutorials on YouTube covering Proteus simulation, sensor interfacing, and embedded C. So far, we have graduated two cohorts of student hardware builders who have produced over 12 functional embedded systems.
